## 3. What “Lifetime Security Maintenance” means

The advertised maintenance benefit refers to covered service assistance **during the supported security lifetime of the supplied phone**, not for the owner's entire lifetime and not beyond the period in which upstream security maintenance is viable.

**Proposed covered service activities** (subject to the final agreement):

1. **Reinstallation:** Reinstall a supported GrapheneOS release and restore the agreed baseline when feasible.
2. **Security updates:** Assist with updates and relevant security configuration checks.
3. **Restoration of settings:** Restore documented security settings that remain supported and appropriate.
4. **Verification:** Recheck agreed configuration and selected platform integrity signals after covered work.

Customers may visit ECS by arrangement or mail the device to an agreed service location. **No public walk-in address or turnaround time is promised in this document.**

## 4. Coverage boundaries to confirm in writing

- Maintenance is subject to hardware functioning and the vendor maintaining viable OS/firmware support.
- No guarantee of protection from all attacks, malware, tracking, interception or social engineering.
- Hardware breakage, batteries, theft, loss, repairs and replacements are not automatically included.
- Shipping, customs, duties, insurance, courier loss and taxes are not automatically covered.
- Third-party subscriptions, licenses, cellular plans and optional products are separate unless quoted.
- Work to recover deleted content, inaccessible accounts or unavailable backups is not automatically included.
- Reinstallation or device reset **may erase all local content**. A backup and consent process is needed.
- App compatibility can change after security updates; alternatives or exceptions may be required.
- The customer is responsible for keeping device ownership and recovery credentials available unless a formal arrangement states otherwise.
- Changes made by the owner or third parties may require reassessment before restoration.
- Acceptable-use, support channels, proof of purchase, identity verification and service response commitments should be stated in the final agreement.

**Maintenance coverage should be made clear before purchase, including what is free, who pays shipping and what happens when the device reaches end of support.**

## 5. Customer responsibilities

- Provide accurate information about mandatory apps and use cases.
- Keep credentials, MFA recovery codes and backups safe.
- Follow agreed security instructions and avoid installing unreviewed apps in sensitive profiles.
- Notify ECS if the phone is lost, stolen, modified, damaged or suspected compromised.
- Avoid sharing passwords, private keys or confidential communications in public GitHub issues or initial enquiries.

## 6. Privacy and confidentiality

Before receiving any customer device, ECS and the customer should agree on minimum necessary access, consent, confidential handling, records, data retention and secure return/shipping procedures. No customer device should be accessed or assessed without proper authorization.
